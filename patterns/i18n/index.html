MO-13 / Multilingual access

One model. Twenty-four languages.

Language access is part of the design. This working example keeps the calculation and your settings together while the words, number formats and plural forms change.

Sampling plan

Change the count or interval. Switching language keeps your values.

Time budget
1.50 s

6 samples

Illustrative timing model: total = count × interval. Bars represent samples; height represents the interval. No measurements are taken.

Complete page coverage

Local and independent

Language packs ship with the page. The selected language is in the URL. No accounts, cookies, preference storage or translation service.
