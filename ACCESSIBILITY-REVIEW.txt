Modern Objectivity — accessibility review
Date: 2026-11-27
Scope: the 36 published style-guide pages, including the accessibility lab.
Reference target: WCAG 2.2 A/AA. This is an engineering review, not a declaration of conformance.

AUTOMATED RESULTS
- axe-core 4.11.0 in Chromium 158.0.8127.0: 194 page/language/theme/state checks, no violations of the selected WCAG A/AA or axe best-practice rules.
- All 36 pages checked in English and German, in light and dark themes; the accessibility lab additionally checked in every other supported EU language.
- Invalid form, success status and open dialog checked separately in both themes.
- 72 layout checks: all 36 pages at 320 CSS pixels, plus independent text enlargement and text-spacing overrides. No horizontal page overflow. Tables may retain their own scroll area.
- Existing regression suite: 1,728 page/language/viewport combinations passed (36 pages, 24 languages, two widths), including state retention, navigation and offline copied templates.
- Catalogue completeness, source extraction reproducibility, font integrity and colour-model tests passed.

INTERACTION CHECKS
Keyboard activation, focus entering and staying inside a modal dialog, Escape, native focus return, the next Tab destination, invalid-field focus, associated error descriptions, live status markup, retained input after language changes, checklist state, system motion preferences and forced-colour focus were exercised in Chromium.
A programmatic accessibility tree and DOM semantics were inspected; actual screen-reader speech was not tested.

ISSUES CORRECTED
- Scrollable tables now have keyboard-focusable, named regions.
- Ten generated handbook task checkboxes now have real labels and can be operated.
- Skipped heading levels in the console and wave experiment were corrected.
- The starter's named summary now has a region role.
- Input, checkbox, switch and range boundaries have stronger contrast.
- Focus colours have stronger contrast on light surfaces and dark navigation.
- Decorative hero rings were subdued to preserve text contrast where they overlap.
- System-colour styling was added for controls and focus indicators.
- The dialog uses native focus restoration, avoiding a delayed second focus change.

SUPPLEMENTARY COLOUR REVIEW
axe reports some gradients, SVG labels and editable text as needing review. These were not counted as automatic passes.
- Brand mark: 8.26:1 against the lighter gradient endpoint.
- SVG label tokens against panel surfaces: 5.84:1 light, 7.64:1 dark.
- Code/input text tokens against secondary surfaces: 5.20:1 light, 6.92:1 dark.
- Hero paragraph text against a conservative composite of the strongest decoration: 4.96:1.
- Essential control boundaries against secondary surfaces: 3.83:1 light, 4.44:1 dark.
- Focus: 4.86:1 on the darker light surface; 10.87:1 on the navigation rail.
Decorative icons hidden from assistive technology carry no unique information. The starter's empty table is an intentional empty state; its populated state is exercised by the existing browser suite.

LIMITS AND FOLLOW-UP
- No hands-on NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or Orca test; no evaluation with disabled participants.
- 320 CSS pixels checks the reflow equivalent of 400% zoom at a 1280-pixel starting viewport. Actual browser zoom and operating-system magnification remain manual checks.
- Automated overflow checks do not establish that every element is unclipped or that every reading order is useful. Desktop/mobile screenshots and representative keyboard journeys were reviewed.
- Browser/device coverage here is Chromium; Safari, Firefox, mobile assistive technology and speech input still need real-device testing.
- Translations are AI-assisted and await independent linguistic review.
- User-selected low-contrast colours in the colour workbench are deliberate experiments, with visible contrast results. Their combinations are not certified.
