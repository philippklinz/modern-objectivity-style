Modern Objectivity / Access

Accessibility lab

Make every action reachable, understandable and recoverable.

WCAG 2.2 AA is the target. These examples and checks are a starting point, not a conformance certificate.

KeyboardClear feedbackEquivalent informationLess movement

Follow the focus

Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move, Enter or Space to activate buttons, and Escape to close the dialog.

Try it

Open the dialog with the keyboard. Focus enters it and returns to this button when you close it.

Sample details

The background is inactive while this dialog is open. Tab stays inside; Escape closes it.

Native controls first

Buttons, links and form controls already have keyboard behaviour. Keep a visible focus indicator and a logical reading order.

<button type="button">Open dialog</button>
<dialog aria-labelledby="dialog-title">
  <h3 id="dialog-title">Sample details</h3>
  <form method="dialog">
    <button>Close dialog</button>
  </form>
</dialog>

Help people recover

Submit the empty form, then correct it. The error names the problem; your input stays intact.

Use at least two characters. This form is a local exercise.

More than a red border

A label, an associated error and an announced result make the form understandable without relying on colour.

<label for="name">Sample name (required)</label>
<input id="name" required
  aria-invalid="true"
  aria-describedby="name-error">
<p id="name-error">Enter at least two characters.</p>
<p role="status" aria-atomic="true"></p>

Let movement be optional

The system preference always wins. The checkbox can reduce movement further.

Keep the meaning available

People may read, listen, enlarge or navigate by headings. The same information must remain available.

Images and charts

Describe an image’s purpose. Decorative images need empty alternative text; charts need a useful summary and accessible data.

Data & tables

Audio and video

Provide captions and transcripts. Describe meaningful visual information that is missing from the audio.

W3C WAI · Audio & video

Structure and language

Use headings, landmarks, table headers and explicit labels. Declare the page language and mark language changes.
